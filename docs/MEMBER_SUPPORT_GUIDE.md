# LUMA WELFARE — MEMBER SUPPORT GUIDE

## Welcome to Luma Welfare

This guide helps you navigate Luma Welfare, your community welfare management platform. If you have questions not covered here, contact our support team.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Registration

1. Visit [luma-welfare.vercel.app](https://luma-welfare.vercel.app)
2. Click **"Join Now"** or **"Register"**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (07XX or 01XX format)
   - Password (minimum 8 characters, must include letters and numbers)
4. Check your email — a 6-digit verification code will be sent. Enter it on the verification page to confirm your address.
5. Sign in to your account

### Activation Fee

After registration, you'll need to pay a one-time **KSh 300 activation fee**:

1. Sign in to your account
2. You'll see the activation fee prompt
3. Click **"Pay Now"**
4. Follow the M-Pesa instructions on screen
5. Enter your M-Pesa PIN when prompted
6. Wait for confirmation

Once paid, your membership is activated and you can browse welfare packages.

## 5. Making Contributions

### How to Contribute

1. Go to your dashboard or **"Contributions"**
2. Click **"Make Contribution"**
3. Select the contribution period
4. Confirm the amount
5. Click **"Pay"**
6. Follow the M-Pesa instructions:
   - Check your phone for the STK prompt
   - Enter your M-Pesa PIN
   - Wait for confirmation

### Payment Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Payment initiated, waiting for confirmation |
| Paid | Payment confirmed and recorded |
| Failed | Payment did not complete |

### What If Payment Fails?

1. **Check your phone** — Did you enter the correct PIN?
2. **Check your M-Pesa balance** — Do you have sufficient funds?
3. **Try again** — If the issue persists, wait 5 minutes and retry
4. **Contact support** — If money was deducted but not reflected

**Important:** Never make duplicate payments without checking your contribution history first.

### Viewing Contribution History

1. Go to **"Contributions"**
2. See all your past contributions
3. Filter by date, status, or package
4. Download statements for your records

## 6. Claims

### Eligibility

Before submitting a claim, ensure:

- [ ] You have an active subscription
- [ ] You've met the waiting period
- [ ] You've made the required minimum contributions
- [ ] Your claim falls within the package benefits

### Submitting a Claim

1. Go to **"Claims"** or click **"Submit Claim"**
2. Select the claim type
3. Fill in the details:
   - Description of the claim
   - Amount requested
   - Supporting documents (if required)
4. Review your submission
5. Click **"Submit"**

### Claim Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Submitted | Claim received, pending review |
| Under Review | Admin is reviewing your claim |
| Approved | Claim approved, payment processing |
| Rejected | Claim not approved (reason provided) |
| Paid | Payment has been sent |
| Info Needed | Admin needs more information |

### Tracking Your Claim

1. Go to **"Claims"**
2. See all your claims with current status
3. Click on a claim to view details
4. Check for any notifications about your claim

### What If My Claim Is Rejected?

1. Check the rejection reason provided
2. Verify if you meet the eligibility criteria
3. If you believe there's an error, contact support
4. You may be able to resubmit with additional information

## 10. M-Pesa Payments

### How M-Pesa Works

Luma Welfare uses M-Pesa for all payments:

1. You initiate a payment on the website
2. An STK push is sent to your phone
3. You enter your M-Pesa PIN
4. Payment is confirmed automatically
5. Your account is updated

### Common M-Pesa Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| STK prompt not received | Check phone signal, try again |
| Payment deducted but not reflected | Wait 5 minutes, check contribution history |
| Wrong PIN entered | Payment fails, try again with correct PIN |
| Insufficient balance | Top up M-Pesa and retry |
| Prompt expired | Wait 2 minutes and try again |

### Payment Security

- **Never share** your M-Pesa PIN with anyone
- **Never pay** to personal numbers — all payments go through the system
- **Always check** your contribution history after paying
- **Report** any suspicious activity immediately

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: What is Luma Welfare?**
A: Luma Welfare is a community welfare management platform that helps you manage subscriptions, contributions, and claims.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email us at [support email] or use the in-app help feature.

### Payments

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Currently, we accept M-Pesa payments only.

**Q: How long does payment confirmation take?**
A: Most payments are confirmed within 30 seconds. If it takes longer, check your contribution history.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Contact support with your transaction details. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.

### Claims

**Q: How long does claim processing take?**
A: Claims are typically reviewed within 48 hours. Complex claims may take longer.

**Q: What documents do I need for a claim?**
A: Requirements vary by claim type. The claim form will specify what's needed.

**Q: Can I cancel a submitted claim?**
A: Contact support to request cancellation before the claim is processed.

### Accounts

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No. Each person should have one account. Contact support if you need to merge accounts.

**Q: What happens if I close my account?**
A: Your historical records are preserved, but you'll lose access to active subscriptions and pending claims.
